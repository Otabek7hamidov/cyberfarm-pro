====================================================================
CYBERFARM PRO - YANGI KOMPYUTERDA ISHLATISH QO'LLANMASI
====================================================================

Ushbu loyiha to'liq portativ qilib yig'ilgan. Unda barcha kerakli vositalar
(LDPlayer 9 emulyatori, ADB platform-tools, ilova APK-si, dinamik QR-skaner,
3 xil Tarmoq/IP rotatsiyasi va Apple HIG Web Dashboard) mavjud.

Batafsil ma'lumot uchun papkadagi YO'RIQNOMA_TO'LIQ.md faylini o'qing.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. TALABLAR:
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit)
2. Virtualizatsiya (VT-x yoki AMD-V) yoqilgan bo'lishi:
   - Tekshirish: Ctrl + Shift + Esc -> Performance -> CPU -> Virtualization: Enabled

--------------------------------------------------------------------
2. ISHGA TUSHIRISH (Faqat 1 ta qadam):
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Papkadagi "run_dashboard.bat" yoki "setup_environment.bat" faylini bosing.
Brauzeringizda avtomatik Boshqaruv Paneli ochiladi:
http://localhost:8501

--------------------------------------------------------------------
3. 3 TA TARMOQ VA IP REJIMI (Chap yon paneldan tanlanadi):
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1) 📱 Mobil Internet (USB Tethering + Parvoz rejimi):
   Telefoningizni USB orqali ulab, telefonda "USB modem"ni yoqasiz.
   Har bir telefonda parvoz rejimi avtomatik yoqib-o'chiriladi va haqiqiy
   O'zbekiston mobil IP-si olinadi. (Eng tavsiya etiladigani).

2) 💻 Kompyuter Tarmog'i (Oddiy):
   Kompyuteringiz mavjud internet ulanishi (Ethernet/Wi-Fi) orqali to'g'ridan-to'g'ri ishlaydi.

3) 🌐 Bepul Avtomatik Proxy (Scraper):
   Internetdagi ochiq bepul proksilarni avtomatik skanerlab ulaydi.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
4. FOYDALANISH:
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Rejani belgilang: "Kunlik maqsad (Limit)" katagiga masalan 50 yozing.
2. Boshlang: "▶️ BOSHLASH" tugmasini bosing.
3. Yangi QR kod tushsa: Chap paneldan rasmni yuklang, havola avtomatik yangilanadi.
